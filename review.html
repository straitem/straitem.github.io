How to install

App Blocker isn’t on the Play Store yet, so your phone will ask a few extra questions. It takes about two minutes. Here is exactly what you’ll see and what to tap.

Already installed it? Skip to the review ↓

  1. 1

    Download the file

    Tap Android Download on the home page. Your browser asks if you trust the site. Tap Download.

  2. 2

    Open it

    When it finishes, tap app-debug.apk in your Downloads. The first time, your phone says it isn’t allowed to install unknown apps. Tap Settings.

  3. 3

    Allow this source

    Switch on Allow from This Source, then press back. You only do this once.

  4. 4

    Tap Install

    Your phone asks Do you want to install this app? Tap Install.

  5. 5

    Let the scan finish

    Your phone scans the app first, then may show Risks May Exist. This is the warning explained below. Tick I acknowledge the risks, then choose the option that lets the install continue. The wording changes between phones: it may say Ignore, More or Install anyway.

  6. 6

    Allow the two permissions

    Open the app. It asks for two permissions and explains each one on screen. Tap Allow on both, and switch on Allow display over other apps when your phone shows that screen.

  7. 7

    If Usage access is greyed out

    Android locks this switch for apps installed from outside the Play Store until you allow it, and says App was denied access. Open Settings → Apps → App blocker, tap ⋮ at the top right, choose Allow restricted settings, then go back and switch on Usage access.

  8. Looks a bit different on your phone?

    Wording and screens vary between Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi, Infinix and other brands, but the steps are the same: allow the download, allow installs from this source, install, then allow the two permissions.

Normal warning

Is the warning a false alarm?

The warning is about where the app comes from, not what is inside it.

Android and the phone’s built-in security scanner warn about every app installed from outside the Play Store. App Blocker is a small independent app that hasn’t been through Google’s review yet, so your phone doesn’t recognise it. That is all the message means.

Look at the scan in step 5. On our test phone the Virus scan line has a tick. The yellow items are Installation source (it didn’t come from the Play Store) and Sensitive permissions. That second one is accurate: an app blocker has to see which app is open and show a lock screen over it, so it asks for exactly those two permissions.

The file is named debug because this is an early version.

Check it yourself

  • The code is public on GitHub, so anyone can read what the app does.
  • It asks for two permissions: Usage access and Display over other apps.
  • You can switch both off in Settings whenever you like, and uninstall the app in one tap.
